natural_earth.bin — offline base map used by the Geopolitics and Maritime maps.

Source: Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), GeoJSON copies from
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (geojson/):
  ne_110m_land, ne_50m_land, ne_110m_lakes, ne_50m_lakes (scalerank <= 5),
  ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land, ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land,
  ne_110m_admin_0_countries (label points), ne_110m_geography_marine_polys (label points),
  ne_10m_ports.

Licence: public domain. "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on
this website are in the public domain. You may use the maps in any manner, including
modifying the content and design, electronic dissemination, and offset printing."
No permission or credit is required; the map shows "Made with Natural Earth".

Rebuild: python -I build_natural_earth.py <dir with the .geojson files> natural_earth.bin

Optional online imagery (SATELLITE / NIGHT LIGHTS basemaps) comes from NASA GIBS
(https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov) — public, no key or account. The map shows
"Imagery: NASA GIBS" while it is on screen, as NASA asks.
